METHODS
Mutation density in knob180, centromeric and intergenic regions

We compared the density of private SNVs (n = 17,115), indels of 1-50 bp (n = 4,854) and
read-validated SVs >50 bp (n = 523; 349 insertions and 174 deletions) in three regions of the J702
assembly (chromosomes 1-10) with the density in randomly shuffled regions of the same size. All
analyses were performed separately for each of the five MA lines, using that line's own SyRI 500 kb
bad bins (9.5-14.5 Mb per line) to define its knob180 blocks and to restrict its shuffles. These are the same bins used to filter the SyRI calls, so no
validated SV lay in its own line's bad bins, and no mutations were removed.

Knob180 regions were built for each line from EDTA knob180 annotations on chromosomes 7 and 8.
Annotations overlapping that line's bad bins or an AGP gap were discarded, and the remaining
annotations <= 100 kb apart were merged into blocks, without merging across a bad bin or gap. This
gave 27-28 blocks (49.2-50.2 Mb) per line. Centromeres were the ten tentative J702 centromere spans
(17.9 Mb), identical for all lines. Intergenic sequence was the remainder of chromosomes 1-10 after
removing AGP gaps, centromeres, that line's knob180 blocks and Liftoff-annotated genes (~1,951 Mb
per line).

To avoid a length bias, in which a multi-kb deletion is more likely than a point mutation to
overlap a region, each mutation was represented by its midpoint. SNVs and insertions were placed at
their position, and deletions at the midpoint of the deleted sequence. Each midpoint was assigned to
one region of its own line in the priority order centromere > knob180 > genic > intergenic.

Expected densities were obtained from 1,000 random shuffles of each region with bedtools shuffle
v2.31.1 (-noOverlapping), performed separately for each line. Every interval could be placed
anywhere on chromosomes 1-10 except that line's bad bins and AGP gaps, and all three regions were
shuffled with the same criteria. Before shuffling, knob180 blocks were cut into consecutive pieces
of <= 1 Mb (74-75 pieces per line, same total length), which prevented very large blocks from being
systematically excluded from chromosome ends. Intergenic intervals were supplied largest-first, with
-maxTries 10000, so that all intervals could be placed; all intervals were placed in every shuffle.
Mutations in a shuffled knob180 region were counted only if they did not fall in a real centromere,
matching the rule for the real knob180. Only a line's own mutations were counted against its real
and shuffled regions.

Density was calculated as mutations per Mb per MA line: the total number of mutations in the region
across the five lines divided by the region size summed over the five lines. Expected density was
the mean density across the 1,000 shuffles. Observed and shuffled densities were compared with a
two-sided empirical permutation test, p = (1 + #{|x_k - m| >= |x_obs - m|}) / (N + 1), where x_obs
is the observed density, x_k is the density in shuffle k, and m is the mean of the N = 1,000
shuffled densities. The smallest attainable p is 1/1,001, reported as p < 0.001.

For graphical representation, pooled observed and shuffled densities were displayed as bars.
Densities were also calculated independently for each of the five MA lines, with individual
estimates represented as dots. Error bars indicate 95% confidence intervals around the mean of the
five line-level estimates, calculated using Student's t-distribution. Analyses used Python 3.10
(NumPy, pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib) and bedtools v2.31.1.


RESULTS
SNVs and indels were depleted in knob180 arrays (0.76 vs 1.55 SNVs per Mb and 0.10 vs 0.44 indels
per Mb; both p < 0.001). Validated SVs were enriched about 2.2-fold (0.104 vs 0.047 SVs per Mb;
p = 0.002). Centromeric densities did not differ from shuffled centromeres (SNVs 1.79 vs 1.56,
p = 0.106; indels 0.32 vs 0.44, p = 0.164; SVs 0.078 vs 0.047, p = 0.279). In intergenic sequence,
SNVs (1.65 vs 1.55) and indels (0.456 vs 0.441) were slightly higher than shuffled, and SVs lower
(0.037 vs 0.048; all p < 0.001).


FIGURE LEGEND
Density of private mutations in J702 knob180 arrays, centromeres and intergenic sequence compared
with randomly shuffled regions. Panels show (left) SNVs (n = 17,115), (middle) indels of 1-50 bp
(n = 4,854) and (right) read-validated SVs >50 bp (n = 523), as mutations per Mb per MA line. Dark
bars show the observed density in the chromosome 7 and 8 knob180 arrays, the tentative centromeres
and intergenic sequence. Light bars show the mean density in the same regions after 1,000 random
genome-wide shuffles with bedtools shuffle, which excluded each line's bad bins and assembly gaps;
knob180 blocks were shuffled as <= 1 Mb pieces. Knob180 blocks and shuffles were defined separately for
each MA line, and each mutation is counted at its midpoint. Densities are total mutations divided by region size
summed over the five lines. Dots, individual MA lines; error bars, 95% t-based confidence intervals
of the line mean. Numbers above bars give the density. Brackets, two-sided empirical permutation
test, observed vs shuffled (1,000 shuffles).
